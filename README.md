# RiskLens

*See Risks. Stay Ahead.*

A web-based Business Health & Risk Intelligence platform that lets SME users submit financial figures and receive an automatically
computed **Business Health Score (BHS, 0–100)**, a **four-tier risk classification**
(Low / Moderate / High / Critical), plain-language recommendations, an interactive dashboard with
historical trends, and a downloadable PDF report. Analytics are rule-based and deterministic,
grounded in the Balanced Scorecard and Altman Z-Score literature.

> Final Year Project — Mohamed Umar Riza, Asia Pacific University.
